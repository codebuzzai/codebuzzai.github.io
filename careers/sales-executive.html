Sales Executive

Full-time Chennai 0–3 years · freshers welcome
Apply now

About the role

CodeBuzz.ai is an AI-powered IT services company. We build web and mobile apps, AI solutions and our own products, like CodeBuzz CRM, for businesses of every size.

We’re looking for a confident, people-loving Sales Executive to talk to customers, understand what they need and show them how our products help their business grow. You don’t need to code — we’ll teach you our products.

What you’ll do

  • Talk to potential customers by phone, WhatsApp, email and in person, and understand their needs
  • Explain and demo our products and services: CodeBuzz CRM, custom web and mobile apps, AI solutions and digital marketing
  • Follow up on leads from our website, ads and referrals, and turn them into happy, paying customers
  • Prepare simple proposals and quotations with the team, and close deals
  • Keep every lead and conversation up to date in our CRM
  • Build long-term relationships, so customers come back and refer others
  • Meet your monthly sales targets, and share what customers ask for with our product team

What we’re looking for

  • Excellent spoken English and Tamil; Hindi is a plus
  • Confident on calls and comfortable meeting business owners
  • 0–3 years in sales, business development or another customer-facing role. Freshers with great communication are welcome
  • Any degree
  • Curious about technology and AI
  • Organised, honest and motivated by targets

Nice to have

  • Experience selling software, SaaS, IT services or digital marketing
  • Your own two-wheeler for client visits

What you get

  • Fixed salary plus performance incentives
  • Training on our products and the AI tools we use
  • Work closely with our leadership team, with room to grow as we grow

How we hire

  1. ApplyFill in the form below. It takes about 3 minutes.
  2. Phone callA short chat about you and the role.
  3. InterviewIncludes a quick sales role-play.
  4. OfferWe move fast for the right person.
