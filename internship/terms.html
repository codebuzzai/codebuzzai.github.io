1. About these terms

These terms apply to the CodeBuzz AI Internship Program (“the program”), run by CodeBuzz.ai (“we”, “us”). They cover registration, payment, participation, certificates, the referral program and Student Ambassadors. “You” means the person registering.

2. Eligibility and registration

  • The program is open to students of diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
  • You must give true, complete and current details. Your name is printed on the certificate exactly as you enter it.
  • One registration per person. We may cancel duplicate or false registrations.
  • Your unique registration ID and portal password are personal. Don't share them.

3. Fees and payment

  • The program fee is shown on the registration and payment pages and is payable in Indian Rupees.
  • Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Students in a scheduled batch must pay to confirm their seat and open the student portal.
  • Students who register with Choose date later may pay at registration or later from the student portal, and choose their batch in the portal from the batches that haven't started yet. Their seat is confirmed only after payment.
  • The optional certificate hard copy is an extra charge (shown on the registration and payment pages), payable together with the internship fee. Like the internship fee, it is non-refundable once paid.
  • If money is deducted but your payment isn't confirmed within a few minutes, write to info@codebuzz.ai with your registration ID and payment reference.

4. No refunds

  • The internship fee, including any certificate hard-copy charge, is non-refundable under any circumstances once the payment is complete — including if you don't attend, leave the program part-way, don't complete it, or are removed under section 6.
  • The fee can't be transferred to another person.
  • If you can't attend your batch, write to us before it starts. We may move you to a later batch, subject to availability and at our discretion.
  • If we cancel or reschedule a batch, we will move you to another batch.
  • Raising a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or card provider for a fee you have paid is a breach of these terms. Your registration and portal access are cancelled, no certificate is issued, and any referral cashback linked to that payment is cancelled.

5. Batches and dates

  • The internship is a virtual program, delivered live online on Zoom. Your Zoom link and session timings are emailed to you before your batch begins.
  • Each batch runs for two weeks. We may change a batch's schedule, timings or format, and will tell you by email if we do.
  • A new batch starts every Monday while batches are open. You can choose a batch when you register, or choose it later in the student portal from the batches that haven't started yet.
  • Once you confirm a batch, it can't be changed from the portal. Write to us if you need help.
  • Joining details for your batch are sent to your registered email before it begins.

6. Participation and conduct

  • Attend the sessions, complete the activities and submit your project as asked.
  • Treat mentors and other students with respect. Harassment, cheating, plagiarism or disrupting sessions may lead to removal from the program.
  • Don't record, copy or share program materials outside the program without our written permission.
  • You need your own laptop or computer, a stable internet connection and the Zoom app to attend the live sessions. Don't share your Zoom link with anyone outside the program.

7. Certificate

  • The CodeBuzz AI internship certificate is issued only to students who complete the program requirements, including attendance and the final project, as communicated during the program.
  • The certificate shows the name you registered with. Contact us before your batch ends if it needs correcting.
  • If you paid for a certificate hard copy, we courier the printed certificate to the home address you registered with, after you complete the program. Courier charges are included. Make sure your address is complete and correct; we aren't responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by an incorrect address.

8. Referral program

  • Every registered student and Student Ambassador gets a personal referral code.
  • You earn the cashback shown on the website (currently ₹100) for each new student who registers with your code and completes a real payment.
  • A student's own cashback is paid only after that student has completed their own payment.
  • Cashback is sent by UPI or bank transfer to the referrer's registered mobile number or account, usually after the referred student's batch has started.
  • You can't refer yourself. Referrals using fake, duplicate or self-created registrations don't qualify. If a referred student's payment is disputed or reversed, the cashback for it is cancelled.
  • We may withhold, cancel or recover cashback where we reasonably believe the program is being misused, and may change or end the referral program at any time. Cashback already earned before a change will still be honoured.

  • Ambassadors earn referral cashback under section 8.
  • Ambassadors must describe the program accurately and must not make promises about jobs, placements or results on our behalf.
  • Being an ambassador doesn't make you an employee, agent or representative of CodeBuzz.ai. We may end an ambassador's participation if these terms are broken.
