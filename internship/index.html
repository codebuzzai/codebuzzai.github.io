FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can apply?

College students from any year of a diploma, undergraduate or postgraduate program can register. No prior coding experience is needed.

Is this a virtual internship?

Yes — it's 100% virtual. All sessions are delivered live on Zoom, so you can join from anywhere. We'll email your Zoom link and session timings before your batch begins.

What do I need to attend?

A laptop or desktop computer, a stable internet connection and the free Zoom app. Keep your camera and microphone working for the live sessions and your project demo. No prior coding experience is needed.

How long is the internship?

Two weeks. A new batch starts every Monday from 16 Nov 2026 to 11 Jan 2027, and each one runs Monday to Sunday of the following week. Pick a batch when you register and the end date is set automatically.

Can I register now and choose my dates later?

Yes. On the registration form, pick Choose date later. After registering you can pay straight away, or skip and pay later from your student portal. Whenever you're ready, log in, choose any upcoming batch and confirm it. A new batch starts every Monday.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every student who completes the program receives a digital CodeBuzz AI internship certificate.

Can I get a printed certificate?

Yes, optionally. Tick Certificate hard copy when you register or pay (₹150 extra, courier charges included). We courier it to your home address after you complete the program.

How do I check my status?

You'll get a separate student login to check your registration, payment and internship status at any time. Log in with the email you registered with.

How does the referral cashback work?

As soon as you register, you get a personal referral code (it's different from your registration ID). When a friend enters your code while registering and completes their payment, you earn ₹100 cashback. You can refer as many friends as you like and track every referral in your student login. Your cashback is sent once your own payment is complete.

What is a Student Ambassador?

Student Ambassadors are alumni who help their juniors discover the program. You get a personal referral code and earn ₹100 cashback for every junior who registers with it and completes their payment — with no limit.

Who can become a Student Ambassador, and is there a fee?

Any alumnus who wants to refer juniors can join. There's no fee and you don't attend the internship. Choose “Student Ambassador” on the registration page — it only asks for your name, email and mobile number.

How do Student Ambassadors get paid?

Your referral code arrives in your welcome email right after you sign up. Log in to the portal with your email to track every junior you refer. Cashback is earned when a junior's payment is complete, and our team contacts you on your registered mobile number to send it by UPI or bank transfer.

Is the internship fee refundable?

No. The fee is non-refundable under any circumstances once paid. If you can't attend, write to us before your batch starts and we may move you to a later batch. See the Terms & Conditions.

What happens after I register?

You'll receive a unique registration ID and a welcome email with your batch details and referral code, and you'll be taken to the payment page. Your seat is confirmed once the payment is complete.
